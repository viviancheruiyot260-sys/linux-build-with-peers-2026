 Reflection

In this module, I learned how Linux manages users, groups, and permissions. I explored important files such as `/etc/passwd`, `/etc/shadow`, and `/etc/group`.

I also practised commands like `id`, `who`, `w`, and `last` to view user information and login activity.

My main takeaway is that managing users and groups helps keep a Linux system secure. I look forward to practising these commands more and improving my Linux skills.
